WHO WE ARE

Bridging Electrical Safety with Clean Power Innovation.

Over a decade ago, our founders recognized a critical flaw in the home improvement industry: general electrical contractors rarely understood solar physics, and fast-talking solar sales companies lacked real electrical master tradesmen.

VoltSolar was built to bridge this gap. Every solar array we design is engineered from the circuit breaker out by fully licensed Master Electricians. We treat your roof, electrical switchboard, and smart home appliances as one unified, high-performance ecosystem.

Our Mission

To empower every family home with resilient electrical safety and self-generated clean solar energy.

Our Promise

Zero high-pressure sales, 100% in-house certified technicians, and full lifetime warranty support.

OUR CARBON MISSION

Driving Long-Term Environmental Impact

We believe that clean energy transition is a responsibility, not just a service. Since our inception in 2016, our team has been dedicated to reducing carbon footprints across our communities, one home at a time. By prioritizing high-efficiency monocrystalline solar systems, we ensure that every square foot of rooftop space delivers maximum environmental return.

Rooftop Carbon Offsetting

Every residential system we commission contributes to local grid stabilization and reduces reliance on fossil-fuel generated power. On average, a standard VoltSolar residential installation offsets approximately 8.2 metric tons of CO2 annually.

Circular Material Life Cycle

We partner with tier-1 manufacturers who adhere to strict circular manufacturing processes. This ensures that the high-density silicon solar panels and lithium storage systems we install are constructed with recyclable materials and designed for end-of-life recovery.

SAFETY & STANDARDS

Rigorous Safety & Regulatory Compliance

Electrical work is inherently high-stakes, which is why safety is our guiding operational core. We don't just aim to meet building codes; we set new compliance benchmarks for our team. Every project undergoes a strict multi-tier review process, from initial 3D structural load modeling to post-installation service panel verification.

Fully Licensed & Accredited

Every technician onsite is a fully licensed professional. Our master electricians oversee each solar utility interconnection and main panel upgrade, ensuring that your home is safe, compliant with national energy codes, and properly permitted.

Continuous Field Auditing

Our Quality Assurance team performs unannounced field audits. We verify physical structural racking anchors, DC wiring isolation conduits, and proper thermal bonding, guaranteeing that your installation remains watertight and structurally sound for decades.
