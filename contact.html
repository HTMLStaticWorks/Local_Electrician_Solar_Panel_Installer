COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear, direct answers to common questions about our services and process.

What areas do you cover for electrical and solar installations?

We service the entire metropolitan region including Central Metro, North Suburbs, West Hills, South Valley, Eastern Foothills, and Coastal Districts. Our dispatch center can route service vans within 2 hours for emergencies.

Are your electricians licensed and insured?

Yes, all our electrical work is performed by fully licensed, background-checked Master Electricians. We conduct code-compliant installations according to strict national electrical standards and carry comprehensive liability coverage.

How long does a residential solar installation take?

The physical installation of panels and electrical inverters on your roof takes between 1 to 3 days. However, the end-to-end process including system design, local council permitting, utility grid approval, and commissioning takes roughly 4 to 8 weeks.

Do you charge for upfront solar quotes?

No, we provide 100% free, no-obligation solar engineering designs, roof shading assessments, and payback estimates. All our proposals feature fixed upfront quotes with zero hidden fees.

What happens in case of an electrical emergency?

We maintain a 24/7 emergency hotline. If you notice severe faults, burning smells, or continuous breaker tripping, please call our emergency dispatch team immediately for immediate priority scheduling.
