METHODOLOGY

How Your Solar ROI is Calculated

Our calculation engine models standard residential performance metrics based on historical irradiance data.

1. Production Modeling

We model generation using high-efficiency 420W+ Tier 1 monocrystalline panels with average regional sun peak hours of 4.2 to 4.8 hours/day.

2. Escalating Utility Rates

Lifetime calculations account for conservative 3% annual grid utility inflation rates that traditional fossil-fuel energy bills experience.

3. Net Metering Assumptions

Assumes approximately 70% immediate daytime home self-consumption and 30% grid export at current standard feed-in tariff credits.

Disclaimer: The calculations provided above are estimated results intended for illustrative and planning purposes only. Exact savings, system size, and payback periods depend on on-site roof shade analysis, pitch orientation, and seasonal energy habits. Estimates are not guaranteed.
