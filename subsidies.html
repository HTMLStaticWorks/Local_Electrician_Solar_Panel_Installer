FINANCIAL INCENTIVES

Understanding Solar Subsidies & Energy Incentives

Learn how government clean energy programs, Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs), and utility net metering can significantly lower your upfront solar investment.

ELIGIBILITY FRAMEWORK

Who May Qualify for Solar Rebates?

Government solar assistance programs generally apply to residential property owners meeting core technical criteria.

Residential Homeowners

Freehold property owners, detached single-family dwellings, and owner-occupier townhouses with approved roof access.

Grid-Connected Properties

Homes connected to the main municipal electricity distribution network that can export surplus energy via bidirectional meters.

Certified Hardware Standards

Incentives require installations carried out by licensed CEC-accredited solar installers using pre-approved Tier 1 components.

REBATE MECHANICS

How Incentives Reduce Your System Cost

Rather than waiting for lengthy tax refunds, most clean energy incentives are applied as an immediate, point-of-sale discount directly on your installation invoice.

1. Small-scale Technology Certificates (STCs)

Certificates created based on expected 10-year clean energy generation, reducing initial invoice cost.

2. Feed-in Tariffs (FiT)

Ongoing bill credits paid by your utility company for every kilowatt-hour of clean power your system sends back.

3. Battery Storage Schemes

Select regional grants offering up to $1,500 – $3,000 for paired home battery installations.
