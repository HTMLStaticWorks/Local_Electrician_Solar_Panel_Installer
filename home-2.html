GOVERNMENT INCENTIVES & REBATES

Make Solar More Affordable.

Depending on your local municipality, grid provider, and property structure, you may be eligible for significant government clean-energy rebates, feed-in tariffs, or clean energy tax credits.

  • Location Specifics: Municipal rebates and regional power grid credits.
  • System Sizing: Incentives scaled directly to array kilowatt capacity.
  • Grid Interconnection: Net metering allows credit for excess solar sent back to the grid.

*Note: Subsidy values and eligibility criteria vary by jurisdiction and official government program rules. Information should always be verified against the latest local guidelines.
