THE SCIENCE OF CLEAN ENERGY

How Residential Solar Works

From photon capture on your roof to powering your morning coffee — here is the streamlined energy flow.

1

Sunlight Capture

Photovoltaic cells absorb photons and generate Direct Current (DC) electricity silently across your roof.

2

Power Conversion

Smart microinverters convert DC power into clean 120V/240V Alternating Current (AC) standard electricity.

3

Home Consumption

Power flows directly to your electrical switchboard, instantly running AC units, appliances, and EV chargers.

4

Storage & Grid Export

Surplus electricity charges your home battery or exports back to the utility grid for power bill credits.
